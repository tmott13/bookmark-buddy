Privacy Policy

Bookmark Buddy (website and Chrome / Edge extension) · Last updated October 19, 2026

The short version: your bookmarks never leave your device. Bookmark Buddy has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no ads. It doesn't collect, sell or share any of your data.

What Bookmark Buddy reads

The titles and links of your bookmarks, and the folders they're in. The extension reads them with the browser's bookmarks permission. The website only reads a bookmarks file you choose to open. This is used for one purpose: sorting your bookmarks into folders.

Where the work happens

All sorting is done by Gemma, an open AI model that runs inside your browser on your own device using WebGPU. Your bookmarks are never sent to Bookmark Buddy, Google, or anyone else. Gemma only sees each bookmark's title and website name, never the full link.

What is stored, and where

  • On your device only: your settings, a backup of where your bookmarks were (so Undo works), which sites go in which folder (so new bookmarks can be sorted), and your in-progress session.
  • The Gemma model is cached in your browser after a one-time download so it works offline.

Uninstalling the extension or clearing site data removes all of it. The “Remove from device” button deletes the downloaded model.

Network requests

The only downloads are the Gemma model files (from Hugging Face) the first time you choose a model. They contain no personal data, and none of your data is sent with them. Bookmark Buddy doesn't fetch favicons or visit your bookmarked pages.

What it can't see

The extension has no access to the web pages you visit, your browsing history, passwords or anything else. It only requests the permissions it needs: bookmarks, storage, notifications, offscreen (to run Gemma in the background) and alarms.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the update will be posted on this page. Questions? Open an issue on GitHub.
